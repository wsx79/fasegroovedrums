**Current release: [v0.15.0](https://github.com/wsx79/fasegroovedrums/releases/tag/v0.15.0).** The existing layout and all controls are retained; common buttons, menus and colours follow the same family style.

**Fase Groove Drums** is the drum companion of
[Fase Groove Piano](https://github.com/wsx79/fasegroovepiano): a VST3/AU instrument that
**writes MIDI** drum parts in the same 13 house genres. Pick a groove (or roll a new one), shape
the feel with swing and humanize, add fills and variations, listen with the built-in guide kit,
then **drag it into your DAW as a MIDI clip** and play it with your own drums.

**Arranger presets:** the style menu now includes thirteen `Arranger: …` entries coordinated with Fase Groove Arranger 0.4.7. Choose the same genre in Piano, Drums and Guitar and use LINK for key, tempo and transport. Jackin follows the requested funky/disco direction, with chopped hooks, articulated bass and percussive guitar. Earlier presets remain available and saved projects retain their settings; DAW levels and effects still contribute to the final mix.

## Features

- 🥁 **70 grooves in 13 genres**, the same as Fase Groove Piano: Piano House, Soulful, Deep,
  Gospel, Jackin', Jackin' Piano, Garage & UK, Afro & Latin, Funky & Disco, '90s Piano House,
  Neo Soul & Broken, Chill & Pads, Dance Stabs.
- 🎲 **NEW BEAT:** a random but musical beat in the genre you choose, from bare to busy (Density).
- 🎚️ **STYLE:** one-click setups by genre (groove, swing, feel, fills, kit) and **RESET**.
- ✏️ **Editable grid:** 13 drums × 32 steps (two bars). Hit, accent, ghost note and flam; drag to
  paint; click a drum's name to mute it; right-click it to change its MIDI note or level.
- 🎲 **Dice everywhere:** total random, new beat, one die per drum, one per part A-D (with locks).
- 🎚️ **VELOCITY view:** a piano roll of velocities, one drum at a time, with shapes and a die: hats that groove.
- 🔊 **Your own samples:** any drum can play your WAV / AIFF / FLAC / MP3 in the guide kit, or load a
  **whole kit folder** at once (samples matched to the drums by their names).
- 🎬 **Song window**, ⏮ ▶ ■ transport, undo / redo, full screen, presets, my grooves, MIDI drum import.
- 🎛️ **Feel:** swing up to the heaviest shuffle (shown as drum machines show it, 50–75%), humanize
  (the kick stays tight, the hands drift) and **NEW FEEL**. In the **MIXER** every drum has its own
  **push / pull** (ms ahead of or behind the beat) and its **share of the swing**; ready-made **TIMING** feels.
- 🧬 **Groove templates:** the timing and accents of an audio loop or a MIDI file, on any groove.
- 🎯 **Chance and rolls** on any step of the grid (right-click): hits that play now and then, quick 2–4 hit rolls.
- ✍️ **FILL view:** draw your own fill; 13 fill styles (kick build, rim shuffle, open hat run, clap stutter,
  percussion break and more).
- 🥁 **Fills** every 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars (snare roll, tom run, clap build, hat lift, drop out,
  conga run, mix), a crash after every fill, and a **variation** every 4 or 8 bars.
- 🎬 **Song sections:** Intro, Main, Break, Build-up, Drop, Outro (automatable), and the
  **whole song** as one clip with markers.
- 🔊 **Guide kit:** four synthesized kits (House 909, Deep 808, Disco Live, Lo-Fi Soul) with
  tone, reverb and volume. No samples: switch it off and play the MIDI with your own drums.
- 📤 **MIDI:** drag the clip into your DAW (loop, 1–16 bars or the whole song, one track or one
  track per drum), save it as `.mid`, or use live MIDI out. General MIDI notes on channel 10 by
  default; every drum's note can be changed.
- 🔉 **Audio export:** the guide sound as WAV: a clip that loops seamlessly, the whole song, or stems (one file per drum).
- 🗂️ **Sample browser:** a folder's samples recognised as drums (by name, folder or sound): listen and pick one per drum.
- 🔗 **LINK:** the same genre, swing, tempo and play button as Fase Groove Piano and
  [Fase Groove Guitar](https://github.com/wsx79/fasegrooveguitar), on the same beat.
- 🌍 **Interface in 6 languages:** English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português (**OPTIONS → Language**).
- ⏱️ **Tempo:** synced to the DAW or set by hand; ▶ plays without the DAW (space bar).

Everything is explained in the **[user guide](docs/GUIDE.md)**.

## For developers

### Building from source

You need CMake ≥ 3.22 and a C++17 compiler (Visual Studio 2022 on Windows, Xcode on macOS).
JUCE 8 is downloaded automatically (or use a local copy with `-DJUCE_DIR=/path/to/JUCE`).

```bash
cmake -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release
cmake --build build --config Release --parallel
ctest --test-dir build -C Release        # engine tests + plug-in tests
```

The built plug-ins are in `build/FaseGrooveDrums_artefacts/Release/`.

On Linux you also need: `libasound2-dev libx11-dev libxrandr-dev libxinerama-dev libxcursor-dev
libxext-dev libfreetype-dev libfontconfig1-dev libxcomposite-dev`.

### Publishing a release

1. Add the new version at the top of [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and
   [CHANGELOG.it.md](CHANGELOG.it.md), and update `VERSION` in `CMakeLists.txt`.
2. On GitHub open **Actions → Build → Run workflow** and enter the version in *release_tag*
   (e.g. `v0.2.0`), or push a tag: `git tag v0.2.0 && git push origin v0.2.0`.

macOS signing and notarization work as in Fase Groove Piano (repository secrets
`MACOS_CERTIFICATE`, `MACOS_CERTIFICATE_PASSWORD`, `MACOS_SIGN_IDENTITY`, `APPLE_ID`,
`APPLE_APP_PASSWORD`, `APPLE_TEAM_ID`); without them the build is signed ad hoc.

### Code layout

```
Source/engine/Drums.*         the kit (lanes, General MIDI notes) and the preset grooves by genre (pure C++)
Source/engine/Generators.*    random beats, variations, song sections, fill styles, arrangement
Source/engine/GrooveEngine.*  the sequencer: swing, humanize, fills, crashes -> note events
Source/GuideDrums.h           the synthesized guide kits
Source/PluginProcessor.*      parameters, tempo sync, MIDI in/out, clip export, user grid
Source/PluginEditor.*         user interface, grid and credits page
Source/Branding.*             logo, name, links
tests/EngineTests.cpp         engine tests (no JUCE dependency)
tests/PluginTests.cpp         plug-in tests: state, grid, styles, MIDI export, DAW transport
```

To add a groove, add a `groove (...)` line to `drumPatterns()` in `Drums.cpp`: one line per drum,
each character a 16th note: `X` accent, `x` hit, `g` ghost note, `f` flam, `.` rest. A one-bar
line in a two-bar groove plays twice. Add new grooves **at the end** of the list, so saved
projects keep theirs.
